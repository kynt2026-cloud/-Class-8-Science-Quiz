Question 1 — Assertion & Reason
Assertion (A): A plant cell placed in distilled water becomes turgid but does not burst.

Reason (R): The rigid cell wall develops an opposing pressure that balances the inward movement of water.
  • A. Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A.
  • B. Both A and R are true, but R is not the correct explanation of A.
  • C. A is true, but R is false.
  • D. A is false, but R is true.
Correct Answer: A
Water enters the plant cell by osmosis, making it turgid. The cell wall exerts wall pressure that prevents the cell from bursting.
Question 2 — Data Interpretation
A student studies three cells and records the following observations.
CellObservation
PMany mitochondria, no cell wall
QCell wall present, large vacuole, no chloroplasts
RNo nucleus, no mitochondria, ribosomes present
Which option correctly identifies the cells?
  • A. P – Plant cell; Q – Animal cell; R – Bacterial cell
  • B. P – Animal cell; Q – Plant cell; R – Bacterial cell
  • C. P – Animal cell; Q – Plant cell; R – Fungal cell
  • D. P – Muscle cell; Q – Leaf cell; R – Virus
Correct Answer: B
P lacks a cell wall and has many mitochondria, so it is an animal cell. Q has a cell wall and large vacuole, indicating a plant cell from a non-green part. R lacks a nucleus but has ribosomes, so it is a bacterial cell.
Question 3 — High Olympiad Challenge
A cell contains a plasma membrane, cytoplasm, ribosomes, and circular DNA. It lacks a nuclear membrane, mitochondria, and chloroplasts. Which statement is most accurate?
  • A. It cannot synthesize proteins because it lacks mitochondria.
  • B. It is a prokaryotic cell capable of independent life processes.
  • C. It is an animal cell in an early stage of development.
  • D. It is a virus because it lacks a nucleus.
Correct Answer: B
The presence of ribosomes and circular DNA without a nuclear membrane indicates a prokaryotic cell, such as a bacterium, which can perform essential life processes independently.
Summary & Key Takeaways
  • Turgidity: Water enters plant cells by osmosis and the cell wall prevents bursting.
  • Animal cells: Lack a cell wall and may contain many mitochondria if energy demand is high.
  • Plant cells: Have a cell wall and a large central vacuole; chloroplasts may be absent in non-green parts.
  • Prokaryotic cells: Lack a true nucleus and membrane-bound organelles but can perform essential life processes independently.
