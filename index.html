Question 1 — Basic Unit of Life
Which of the following is defined as the basic structural and functional unit of all living organisms?
  • A. Tissue
  • B. Cell
  • C. Organ
  • D. Organ System
Correct Answer: B. Cell
Explanation: Just as bricks are assembled to make a building, cells are assembled to make the body of every living organism. Hence, the cell is the basic structural and fundamental unit of life.
Question 2 — Cellular Powerhouse
Which cell organelle is responsible for generating energy through respiration and is known as the "Powerhouse of the Cell"?
  • A. Chloroplast
  • B. Ribosome
  • C. Mitochondrion
  • D. Endoplasmic Reticulum
Correct Answer: C. Mitochondrion
Explanation: Mitochondria produce cellular energy in the form of ATP (Adenosine Triphosphate) through cellular respiration, earning them the title "Powerhouse of the Cell".
Question 3 — Plant vs Animal Cell
Which structure provides rigidity and shape to plant cells, but is completely absent in animal cells?
  • A. Plasma Membrane
  • B. Nucleus
  • C. Cell Wall
  • D. Cytoplasm
Correct Answer: C. Cell Wall
Explanation: Plants require protection against variations in temperature, high wind speed, atmospheric moisture, etc., because they cannot move. The outer rigid layer outside the cell membrane is called the Cell Wall.
Summary & Key Takeaways
Key Concepts for Class 8 Cell Biology:
  • Discovery: Robert Hooke observed cells in a cork slice in 1665.
  • Plant Cells: Have a Cell Wall, Chloroplasts (for photosynthesis), and a Large Central Vacuole.
  • Animal Cells: Lack Cell Walls and Chloroplasts, and contain small, temporary vacuoles.
  • Prokaryotes vs Eukaryotes: Organisms lacking a well-defined nuclear membrane (like bacteria) are Prokaryotes; those with a true nucleus are Eukaryotes.
